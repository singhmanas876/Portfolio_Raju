Quality & Export Process

Quality Checks

Every lot is checked before dispatch for grain size, colour, aroma, moisture and cleanliness. Samples can be shared with buyers for approval before an order is confirmed.

Packaging

Rice is packed in food-grade bags (such as PP or non-woven bags) in sizes agreed with the buyer, with buyer labelling or private-label packing where required, and loaded safely into containers.

Shipping Terms

We can support common terms such as FOB, CFR and CIF, depending on the order and destination port.

Documents

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and other documents such as certificate of origin or phytosanitary certificate, as required by the buyer and destination country.
